Getting to Solapur

SSE
Solapur Airport — about 10 minutes away Small domestic airport on the same road as the hotel. Connects to Mumbai, Hyderabad and Goa.
BOM
Mumbai — your likely international landing Connect onward by short flight, overnight train, or a road transfer of roughly 6 to 7 hours.
PNQ
Pune Airport — the closest big-city option Around 4 to 5 hours by road, and an easy train ride if you would rather not drive.
RAIL
Solapur Junction — a major stop Well connected to Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad and Bengaluru. Often the smoothest way in.
