Getting ready for India प्रवासाची तयारी

December weather

Mumbai

Warm and humid even in December — highs near 30–32°C, evenings around 22–24°C. Light, breathable fabrics work all day; keep a thin layer for the evening sea breeze.

Pune

Pleasant and dry, but mornings and nights get genuinely cool — think 10–14°C before sunrise, climbing to a comfortable 27–29°C by afternoon. Pack a light jacket or shawl.

Solapur (the venue)

Dry inland heat by day (30–32°C) with a bigger swing after dark — early mornings for the Vidhi and late nights at the Sangeet can dip to 14–16°C. Sunscreen by day, a wrap after sunset.

Before you land

Visa

Most visitors need an Indian e-Visa — apply online well ahead of your flight (a few weeks is safest). Check the requirements for your passport and keep a copy of the approval with you.

Money & cards

Cards work in most cities, but carry some rupees in cash for smaller shops and markets. Let your bank know you're traveling, and withdraw from ATMs rather than exchanging cash at the airport.

Power & SIM

India runs on 230V with Type C/D/M sockets — bring a universal adapter. Grab a local eSIM or prepaid SIM (Airtel or Jio) at the airport for easy data; you'll want it for maps and rides.

Shopping in Pune & Mumbai

Where to browse

In Mumbai: Colaba Causeway and Fashion Street for accessories and quick finds, Linking Road for clothes, Zaveri Bazaar for jewelry. In Pune: FC Road and Laxmi Road for traditional wear and footwear.

Bargain where it's a market

Street markets expect haggling — starting around half the first price quoted is normal. Malls, FabIndia, and branded stores are fixed-price, no bargaining needed.

Plan for tailoring

A blouse or kurta stitched to fit usually takes 3–5 days. If you want something custom-made for the wedding, shop early in your trip rather than the day before.
